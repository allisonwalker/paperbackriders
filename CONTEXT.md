# Paperback Riders

The public site for the Paperback Riders book club: what the club is reading, when it meets next, and what it has already read.

## Language

**Pick**:
One club month. Either a specific book or a prompt.
_Avoid_: Book, event

**Current pick**:
The pick the catalog shows at the top and labels Current pick. The history marks that pick, including before its month starts. It stays current while its meeting is scheduled or unscheduled, and while no meeting is saved. After this meeting has happened or been marked skipped, the next saved pick becomes current, even before that month starts. A further pick waits.
_Avoid_: Featured pick, this month's pick, November's pick

**Reached**:
The current pick and every earlier pick. Totals and pins use these picks. A pick beyond the current one waits. When the catalog is unselected, every saved pick is reached. The calendar month does not decide this.
_Avoid_: Calendar month

**Unselected**:
No current pick. This is the stretch after a meeting has happened or been skipped, before the next pick is saved. The catalog reads "The next book hasn't been selected. Watch this space." The history marks no row. A club that has never saved a pick is not this.
_Avoid_: No upcoming meeting

**No upcoming meeting**:
No meeting is saved. The catalog says "No upcoming meeting is set."
_Avoid_: Skipped, unscheduled

**Skipped**:
Allison's choice that this pick will not have a meeting. The choice is remembered on that pick. The site does not announce it. The following saved pick becomes the current pick, and the next meeting discusses that pick with the date, time, and place cleared. With no following pick, the catalog is unselected.
_Avoid_: Missed, no meeting

**Next meeting**:
The club's one meeting. It discusses the current pick. It is scheduled or unscheduled. With none saved, the catalog has no upcoming meeting.
_Avoid_: Event, this month's meeting

**Happened**:
A pick remembers that its organized meeting's date has passed. Saving the next meeting does not erase that memory. Members do not see a list of past meetings.
_Avoid_: Missed

**Scheduled**:
A next meeting whose date, time, and place are known. While that date is still today or ahead, its pick is the current pick. On the next club day, the meeting has happened.
_Avoid_: Set, confirmed

**Unscheduled**:
A next meeting whose date, time, and place are unknown together. It discusses the current pick and keeps that pick current. Members read "Date and place to come," then the book or the prompt.
_Avoid_: Fuzzy, tentative, TBD
